Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Puzzling: Word Puzzle Game and its third-party partners collect, use, and share information about you when you use the word game application. By downloading or playing Puzzling: Word Puzzle Game, users consent to the collection and use of your information as described here.

1. Information Collection and Use
We collect certain technical and usage data—such as device identifiers and gameplay statistics—to improve the game experience and ensure our services run correctly.

2. Advertising Partners
To display advertisements, we share non-personal information (for example, your device’s advertising ID) with our advertising partners. We do not disclose any personally identifiable information to advertisers.

3. Game Progress Backup
If you choose to back up your game progress, we use your account information to allow you to sign in on multiple devices and restore your progress.

4. Data Storage
When you sign in to back up your progress, your data is stored on Unity’s servers. You may delete this data at any time via the in-game settings.

5. Policy Updates
We reserve the right to modify this Privacy Policy at any time. Any changes will take effect immediately upon posting.

6. Contact
For any inquiries, please contact us at: contact@supportpuzzling.com.
